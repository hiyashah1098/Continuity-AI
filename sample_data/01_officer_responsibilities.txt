Continuity Collective Officer Responsibilities

President: chair weekly leadership meetings, submit the annual room reservation by August 20, and confirm the event approval packet is complete before public promotion.
Treasurer: maintain the budget, collect receipts, submit the fall budget proposal by September 15, and reconcile the account monthly.
Event Chair: draft event plans, confirm vendors, submit event approval materials 30 days before each event, and maintain the event checklist.
All officers: store final records in the shared drive using YYYY-MM-DD_topic naming.
